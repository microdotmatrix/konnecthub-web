# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers, in this order:

1. **Ecommerce operators and the agencies that run their stacks** (Shopify, BigCommerce). They arrive with a concrete pain: store, inventory, CRM and fulfilment systems that do not talk to each other, or automations that fail without anyone noticing. They give the page seconds to prove KonnectHub can take that work off their plate, and decide whether to talk to the team.
2. **Developers at those merchants and agencies** who will build on it. They want proof it is real infrastructure: TypeScript flows, sandboxed workers, versioned deployments, readable executions, a CLI that deploys from CI.

## Product Purpose

KonnectHub is a hosted runtime for integration flows. Teams write a flow in TypeScript or build it on a canvas, deploy it with its own inputs and limits, and attach triggers (webhooks, schedules, event sources). Every firing is recorded as an event, and every run can be opened and read step by step. Platform services such as Shopify ship with managed credentials, event topics and webhook registration. The KonnectHub team also builds integrations for clients (done-for-you services).

The marketing site turns a visitor into a conversation. Primary action: contact the team or book a call about an integration. Secondary: start a workspace. Success is qualified service inquiries, and developers who trust the platform enough to recommend it.

## Brand Personality

Bold, fast, loud. Mechanical and exact underneath. The site feels like a machine running in plain sight: events firing, runs ticking, nothing hidden. Confidence comes from showing the machine, not from adjectives. The docs voice (precise, honest about edge cases) is the floor: every claim stays true to how the platform behaves.

## Anti-references

- **Zapier / Make clones**: pastel app-icon grids, "connect 6,000 apps" tropes, cartoon blobs.
- **Generic SaaS template**: centered hero with gradient blob, three icon cards, logo strip, hero-metric stats.
- **Corporate enterprise**: navy palettes, stock handshake photos, safe gray copy, "solutions" language.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the machine.** Every claim is backed by a live, animated piece of the product behaving the way the docs say it does.
2. **Loud surface, exact substance.** Type and motion carry the energy; the words stay literal and true.
3. **Ops story first, engineering depth second.** Lead with outcomes an operator feels; developers find the depth by scrolling.
4. **Nothing is lost, nothing is hidden.** Held deliveries, one event per firing, step-level runs: the platform's honesty is the brand promise. Make it visible.
5. **Every section earns the next scroll.** One dominant idea per fold. Pacing over density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every animation has a static reduced-motion equivalent that still tells the story. Status is never shown by color alone. Interactive demos work by keyboard.
